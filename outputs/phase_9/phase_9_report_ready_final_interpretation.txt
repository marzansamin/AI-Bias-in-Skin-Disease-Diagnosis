============================================================
FINAL RESULTS — PAPER-READY INTERPRETATION
============================================================

INTERNAL DATASET — FITZPATRICK17K
------------------------------------------------------------

The internal evaluation compared the Baseline, M1, M2, and
M1 + M2 configurations using the available Fitzpatrick17k
performance records.

The strongest internal result should be reported using the
metric-specific best model rather than assigning one model
as universally optimal.

The available results indicate that mitigation effects differ
across metrics. Therefore, Accuracy, Balanced Accuracy and
Macro-F1 should be interpreted separately.


EXTERNAL DATASET — DDI
------------------------------------------------------------

The external evaluation compared Baseline, M2, M3 and M2 + M3
under the harmonized 14-class disease space.

M2 achieved the highest DDI Accuracy and Balanced Accuracy.

M2 + M3 achieved the highest DDI Macro-F1 among the evaluated
models.

However, the M2 + M3 model mapped only 41.90% of DDI
predictions to the harmonized prediction space, while
122 of 210 predictions were outside that space.

Consequently, the external performance results should be
treated as evidence of experimental cross-dataset behavior,
not clinical readiness.


R3 REPRESENTATION BIAS
------------------------------------------------------------

The Baseline R3 probe balanced accuracy was 60.42%.

M3 reduced R3 probe balanced accuracy to 46.03%, corresponding
to a 14.39 percentage-point reduction.

M2 + M3 achieved an R3 probe balanced accuracy of 52.14%,
corresponding to an 8.28 percentage-point reduction relative
to Baseline.

Therefore, M3 provides the strongest direct reduction in
skin-tone representation predictability.


PERFORMANCE–FAIRNESS TRADE-OFF
------------------------------------------------------------

No single mitigation dominates every performance and fairness
criterion.

M2 provides strong external balanced accuracy, whereas M2 + M3
provides the strongest external Macro-F1.

M3 provides the strongest R3 representation-bias reduction.

This demonstrates that diagnostic performance and
representation-bias mitigation should be evaluated jointly
rather than through a single performance metric.


GENERALIZATION
------------------------------------------------------------

Available internal-to-DDI comparisons show substantial
performance degradation from Fitzpatrick17k to DDI.

This indicates that performance obtained on the internal
dataset does not automatically transfer to the external
dataset.

The generalization analysis should therefore be presented as
evidence of domain-shift sensitivity rather than as evidence
of universal model failure.


OVERALL CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------

The results support the usefulness of evaluating diagnostic
performance, fairness, and representation bias jointly.

M2 is strongest for several DDI diagnostic metrics.

M3 is strongest for R3 representation-bias reduction.

M2 + M3 provides the highest DDI Macro-F1 among the evaluated
models, but its substantial out-of-space prediction rate
requires cautious interpretation.

The framework therefore demonstrates measurable mitigation
effects, while also revealing substantial challenges in
cross-dataset generalization and label-space compatibility.
